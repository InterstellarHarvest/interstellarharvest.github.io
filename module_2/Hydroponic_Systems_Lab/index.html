How to Play

Want to see the interface walkthrough again?

Reservoir
Water supply for the entire system. Every system needs at least one.
Pump
Pushes water through the system. Water enters one side and exits the other.
Grow Channel
Where plants grow! Needs water flow, LED light above, and nutrients in the water. The heart of your farm.
Fan
Air circulation. Prevents CO2 buildup around leaves. Must be placed within 2 cells of a grow channel.
LED Panel
Provides light for photosynthesis. Place 1–2 rows above a grow channel. Click-hold to set spectrum color.
Nutrient Injector
Feeds nutrients into water flow. Supply depletes over time — watch the screen color! Click-hold to set concentration levels.
Pipes
Straight, Elbow, T-Joint, and Cross. Route water between components. Click to rotate.
pH Sensor
Monitors water chemistry. Place inline in the pipe path to enable pH and nutrient readings in the status bar.

Drag & Place

Drag any component from the Parts Bin on the right and drop it onto the workspace grid. Each level provides a limited set of parts — plan your layout before placing!

You can also move placed parts to reposition them. Drop a part outside the grid to return it to the bin.

Rotate Parts

Click any rotatable component to rotate it 90°. Pumps, pipes, nutrient injectors, and pH sensors all have directional ports that must face neighboring parts to connect.

Configure Components

Click-hold (long press) on LED Panels to choose a light spectrum. Each plant species has a preferred spectrum — matching it gives a significant growth boost.

Click-hold on Nutrient Injectors to set concentration level. Higher concentration feeds plants faster but depletes the supply sooner. The injector's screen shows remaining supply.

Connect Ports

Red dots mark unconnected ports. Use pipes to link components into a complete water circuit. When two ports touch they connect automatically.

Reservoir → Pump → Grow Channel

Nutrient injectors and pH sensors are inline components — place them between pipes in the water path, not off to the side.

Removing Parts

  • Right-click any placed part to remove it (returns to bin)
  • Use the Clear button in the parts bin header to remove everything at once

Starting the Simulation

Press the POWER button in the bottom status bar to start. Water flows through the pipes, LEDs light up, fans spin, and plants begin growing day by day. Each real-time second equals one growing day.

Press Power again to stop the simulation and return to building mode. All plant progress resets when you stop — so make sure your system is ready before powering on.

Status Bar Readings

  • Flow — Check for gaps, ERR means your pipe connections are broken.
  • pH — Water acidity level. Requires a pH Sensor in the flow path. Most plants thrive at pH 5.5–6.5. Without nutrients, pH drifts to neutral (7.0).
  • Light — The active LED spectrum. Match your plant's preference for the best growth multiplier. Wrong spectrum still works, just slower.
  • Nutrients — Current nutrient concentration. Requires a pH Sensor to read. Shows NONE when injectors are depleted.
  • Harvest — Estimated days until maturity. Color shows health: green = thriving, yellow = stressed, red = dying. Shows READY at full maturity.
  • Day — Current simulation day counter.

During Simulation

The System Log shows real-time messages about system performance, plant growth milestones, and science facts about space agriculture.

You can open Help or the Field Guide during simulation — the timer pauses and resumes automatically.

The simulation ends when plants reach full maturity (harvest!) or when the maximum growth period expires. Either way, you'll see your rating and results.

Scenario Results

When the simulation ends, you receive feedback based on how your system performed:

  • Strong result — Full maturity, health above 75%. Optimal conditions throughout.
  • Crop reached maturity — Plants grew, but health was lower than ideal. Check light spectrum, nutrients, and airflow.
  • Partial growth — Crop did not reach maturity. Review your system design and try again.
  • No establishment — Plants did not grow. Check water flow, LED placement, and pipe connections.

All scenarios are available from the start. You can revisit any scenario using the selector in the title bar.

Pro Tips

  • • Check the Field Guide — Each plant has different light, nutrient, and airflow preferences. Open the Field Guide button to learn exactly what your current crop needs.
  • • LED placement — LEDs work best 1–2 rows directly above grow channels. Too far away and plants get no light at all.
  • • Airflow matters — Fans within 2 cells of a grow channel provide circulation. Without it, CO2 pockets form around leaves.
  • • Nutrient balance — High concentration depletes in ~10 days, Medium in ~15, Low in ~25. Plan around your plant's growth cycle length.
  • • Watch the injector screen — The colored screen on nutrient injectors shows remaining supply at a glance. Green = plenty, yellow = running low, red = nearly empty.
  • • Efficiency counts — Getting plants to maturity faster (close to the ideal growth days) earns a higher efficiency score.
